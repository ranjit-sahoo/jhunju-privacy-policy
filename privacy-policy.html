Privacy Policy

This app works fully offline. There is no account or login, and it does not send or receive any data over the internet.

We do not collect any personal information from children or parents - no name, email, phone number, location, or photos. The child's nickname, stars and learning progress are saved only on the device and are never sent anywhere.

No ads, no analytics or tracking, no third-party SDKs, no in-app purchases, no external links.

Speech narration uses your phone's own text-to-speech and audio built into the app; nothing reaches us.

Microphone (voice recording): in "Say it & hear", the child's voice is recorded temporarily on the device only and played right back. Recordings are never saved, never sent anywhere, and are erased as soon as the screen changes or the app closes. Mic permission is requested only for this feature, at the moment of use.

Deleting your data: the parent zone has an "Erase all app data" button that removes the child's progress, stars and settings. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything from the device. We run no servers, so nothing else remains to delete.

Children's privacy: the app collects no data, shows no ads, and does no tracking.

Data processing: the child's nickname, stars, progress and the temporary microphone audio are processed on this device only and never leave it. No personal data is collected, shared, or sent anywhere.

Terms of Use

This app is free educational entertainment for children aged 2-8. All rhymes, artwork and activities are original and made only for this app; stories are original retellings of classic public-domain tales. Illustrations and voice narration are AI-generated (synthetic).

The app is provided "as is"; learning outcomes are not guaranteed. Parents are responsible for setting screen-time limits and supervising their child.
